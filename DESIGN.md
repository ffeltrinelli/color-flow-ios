# Color Flow visual design

Color Flow uses a warm cartoon style inspired by the Cozy Pour launcher and splash artwork. Rounded silhouettes, dark outlines, and simple shadows give the pieces depth. In gameplay, matching colors, paint quantities, and the next pour take priority over decoration.

## Palette and controls

- Use deep navy behind the puzzle. Use warm cream and dark ink for panels and text, gold for primary actions and the level badge, and teal for accents.
- Keep red, green, blue, yellow, and purple consistent across containers, liquid, buckets, tins, and hint controls. Shade from the shared paint palette so a color still reads the same across surfaces.
- Use rounded type, with heavier weights for headings, actions, and paint quantities. Keep control silhouettes simple. Buckets have handles, reserve tins have lids, and a lock indicates a blocked bucket. Keep quantities readable in each state.

## Cozy Enamel boards

- Treat square boxes and circular sectors as shallow enamel trays. Use warm dark contours, a cream glint along the rounded rim, and a darker tinted well. Leave clear seams around each container and preserve the shape of open space.
- Keep circular rims slim and corners lightly rounded so small sectors read at the iPhone SE scale. Curve the rim, shading, and fill with the sector. Keep the center hub and the entry pointer visually distinct.
- Give paint a satin finish with a fine glint inside the visible fill edge. An empty well should be clearly darker than even a small amount of paint. On the square board, paint rises from the bottom. On the circular board, it spreads outward from the inner edge. The area of the band shows the fill fraction.

## Motion and readability

- Draw the pour as a narrow continuous ribbon with a darker edge and a fine highlight. Match the receiving paint color and join the bucket lip to the moving fill surface. Keep the circular pointer fixed. Show the stream once the entry lane aligns beneath it.
- Keep gameplay motion tied to the pour, fill, removal, and fall. Use the lighter Reduce Motion presentation for players who enable it. The launcher and hint river can use larger illustrated splashes because they sit outside the puzzle.
- Review dense and partially cleared boards at 375 × 667 points and on a larger iPhone. Check the smallest circular sectors, thin partial fills, open lanes, and bucket and tin quantities. Keep interactive targets and VoiceOver labels available independently of the artwork.
