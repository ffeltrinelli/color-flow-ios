# Aphorism sources

Color Flow bundles 100 short excerpts from historical editions. The game displays the author and original work. This bibliography credits the editions used to check wording. Line breaks and punctuation were adjusted where needed for onscreen reading.

55 entries were checked against John Bartlett’s [*Familiar Quotations*](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/16732), in the transcription produced by Chuck Greif and Pat Saumell.

The remaining 45 entries were checked against these Standard Ebooks editions.
